# First XI Cricket (HTML)

A single self-contained HTML cricket career sim, ported from the Excel/VBA "First XI" workbook. Every ODI from Jun 2002 to Jan 2026 (Cricsheet fixtures, real XIs and real bowling plans) played ball by ball with the First XI run-rate cards, plus Test cricket. No framework, no runtime dependencies. `npm run build` bundles `src/` + `data/world.json` into **`dist/first-xi.html`** - that one file is the product.

## No Node on your machine?
The repo root holds a prebuilt `first-xi.html` (double-click to open). **After any source or data change, rebuild and copy `dist/first-xi.html` to the repo root before committing** (`npm run check` fails if it's stale).

## The rules (sacred - this is the board game)
- Each over: a run-rate card RR_3 / 4A / 4B / 5 / 6 / 7 is picked by weighted random from the bowler's rating (`CARD_WEIGHTS[fmt][rating]`), shifted by the situation (`situationShift`, port of `CalcSituationShift`; `applyShift` ports `ApplyShift` including its lossy overflow).
- One roll 1-100 per over picks the row; balls 1-6 read columns B-G. A wide/no-ball is re-bowled off a fresh roll, column 1 (`M.cur.comp`).
- Wicket colours (`wicketCounts(colour, cardColour)`): a BLUE batter falls only to BLUE, GREEN to BLUE/GREEN, RED to anything; otherwise the ball is a dot (`ball.voided`, `ball.bcol`).
- Batting colours (`batColour`): classic rule = by position (1-6 BLUE, 7-9 GREEN, 10-11 RED; Settings → Batting colours → Classic, `M.batMode='position'`). Player colours (default): one letter, or two = vs associates + vs Test nations (`BG`). A colour from the workbook's BatGroup or the player card applies exactly; a derived colour is never weaker than his position, except an associate's second letter against a Test nation. Derived in `extract.py` `bat_group`: usual position ≥10 RED, 7-9 GREEN; full-member top order BLUE with `BAT_BLUE_CAPS` (20) caps else GREEN; associate top order BLUE with `BAT_BLUE_VS_FULL` (40) games against full members, else `BG`. Tuned with `tools/dev/strength.js`: full members beat associates ~86% (classic rule: ~50%), full v full and associate v associate score as in classic; associates batting first v Test nations average ~157. Turning associates' lower order RED v Test nations as well overshoots.
- Era (default; Settings → Ratings → Whole career turns it off): `players[pid][7]` = `[[year, rating, colour], ...]`. `pAttr(pid, date)` reads the entry for the match date (`M.date`; UI default `careerDate()`). Ratings = last `ERA_YEARS` (3) years of spells steadied by `ERA_PRIOR_BALLS` (300) of his earlier record, debutants on their first spells; colours count caps/vs-full games *before* that year (regulars of the first data season get `ERA_PRIOR_CAPS` 20). Hand ratings/BatGroups win for every year.
- Striker is always the one out. Next batter = batting order[wkts+1]. Odd runs (incl. byes) swap strike on legal balls; end of over swaps.
- Bowling engine (`bowlEngInit`/`chooseBowler`/`bowlEngEndOver`, port of `modBowlingEngine`): ODIs follow the real Cricsheet bowling plan per over (`M.plans`, from BowlingXI OverList) and fall back to the spell engine (spell/rest lengths `SPELLS`, phase bonus, quality, random 0-15). Max 10 overs, no consecutive overs.
- MOTM (`pickMOTM`, port of `modMOTM`): runs×SR/100 + wkts×25 + maidens×10 − econ×3 + ct 10 / st 15 / ro 12 + 50/100/5w bonuses 15/30/40, ×1.25 for the winners.
- Milestones (`MS_DEFS`, port of `modMilestones`): logged once per `fmt:pid:cat:thr` key (`S.msKeys` - normalised ids, so they never re-fire). Fastest 50/100 are checked live at the exact ball (`liveFastest`), never from post-match totals; the first one sets the benchmark and records count after 10 matches in a format.
- Tests are new (Excel only had an MVP): `CARD_WEIGHTS.TEST` (RR_3 most overs, RR_7 the wicket card), 450 overs, 2-over changeovers, weather (`WEATHER_P`), pitches (`PITCHES`, by home region), wear from day 4 (`TEST_WEAR`), innings pressure (`TEST_INN_PRESSURE`), follow-on at 200, AI declarations (`aiShouldDeclare`). Tuned with `tests/engine-stats.js` to ~34 runs a wicket, ~3.4 an over, ~12% draws. Change those constants only with that script.

- Captaincy (Settings → Captaincy → Me; `M.manualCaptain` = your followed team if playing, else both, from `captainOf`): `M.nextBowler` (consumed by `captainBowler` at the start of an over; options and rules in `bowlerOptions`: anyone but the keeper, not the last over's bowler, max 10 ODI overs; part-timers join `eng.B` as rating ≤6), `setNextBatter` (reorders who's still to bat), attitude `M.att[batTeam]` / `M.att['f'+bowlTeam]` ±1 added in `situationShift`. A position can lift a derived batting colour one step at most (no BLUE No.11 by opening him). Test declarations/follow-on are yours too.

- Rankings & awards (`07b-rankings.js`, state `S.rk` from `blankRankings`, rebuilt by `rebuildRankings` for old saves): team Elo per format (`RANK_K`, start 1500 / 1400 ZIM-BAN / 1250 associates, shown as `(elo-1000)/5`), player rankings = exponential average (`RANK_ALPHA`) of match performance × opposition strength, scaled by `PLAYER_SCALE`, active within `RANK_ACTIVE_DAYS`; `playerOfSeries` when a series has no fixtures left (`matchScores` = MOTM formula without the winner bonus); `yearEnd` awards every finished year once (`awardsFor`, min `AWARD_MIN_MATCHES`), composite XIs excluded. Called from `onMatchDone`; UI `12b-ui-rankings.js`.

- Presentation (`12c-fx.js`): `FX.allow` is set by `step()` (true for a single ball / one over / watch, false for 5 overs, innings, sim) - graphics (`tvBanner` via `fxEvent`, from `uiMatchHook`; Settings → TV graphics) and sound (`SFX`, WebAudio synthesised, `fxBall`; off by default, 🔇 button on the match screen) only play then. `fxDice` tumbles a new over's roll (fixed sequence, not `Math.random`, so seeded tests stay repeatable). Engine fires a `w` event per wicket. `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.

- Rain & D/L (limited overs, `03-engine.js`): `rollRain` at the toss (`RAIN_P` by region) sets `M.rainAt {inn, over, lost}`; `applyRain` at the end of that over cuts `I.maxOv` (per-innings overs; everything limited-overs reads `I.maxOv`, not `M.maxOv`), keeps `M.dl {r1, r2, max2, target}` with resources from `dlRes(u,w)` (a fitted approximation of the D/L table, `DL_B`/`DL_F`), and `dlTarget` revises the target (G50 when the chasing side has more). Under 20 overs possible (5 in a T20) → `M.abandoned`, no result. Knockout ties (`stage` final/semi/quarter/play-off, or `M.superOverOn`) → `superOver` (one over each, two wickets, three rounds, then boundary count), result type `'so'` with a winner. Archive: `E.maxOv`, `inns[].mo`, `E.dl`, `E.so`, `E.abandoned`.

- Formats: `FORMATS` = ODI, TEST, T20 (`perFmt` builds per-format tables; old saves gain missing ones in `loadSlot`). `LIMITED(M)` = not a Test; `perBowler(M)` = 10 / 4 overs. T20 cards: `T20_CODES` ladder RR_4B/5/6/8/9/7 with two PROVISIONAL derived cards (`deriveCard`: RR_8 from RR_6, RR_9 from RR_7, same wicket tokens, a fixed pattern of upgrades), `CARD_WEIGHTS.T20` tuned to ~155 / 6.2 wkts (`node tests/engine-stats.js 0 0 400`). Workbook RR_8/RR_9 sheets would replace them. Series you schedule (`S.tests`, any format, `createSeries`, keys `T<n>.<k>`, `customFx`): T20Is from `T20_START`.

- Coach career & squads (`07c-coach.js`, state `S.coach` from `newCoach`, `S.squads`, `S.inj`, `S.form`; UI `12d-ui-coach.js`): `ensureObjectives` sets a board target per series from the rankings gap (winSeries / notLose / winOne / reach stage / winN) plus a yearly ODI-rankings target (`yearObjective`, only once there's a table); `judgeObjectives` when the series is done (`seriesDone`); confidence ±2-4 a result, ±14/16 a target, drifting back to 55; sacked below `COACH_SACK`, renewal at `COACH_RENEW` when the contract (`CONTRACT_DAYS`) ends; `offersFor` down/up/any; `C.stop` halts a bulk sim. `managedTeam()` (coach team, or followed team with Settings → Selection → Me) gets `squadFor` (15) and `pickXI` (real XI if in squad and fit, else like-for-like), `rollInjuries` after each match, `fatigueOf` (≥60 → a bowling rating step). Form for everyone (`updateForm`, `formLevel`, role-relative thresholds `FORM_COLD`/`FORM_HOT`/`FORM_BOWL`, balanced ~1 hot / 1 cold batter a match); `matchMods` → `M.mods` read by `batColour`/`bowlEngInit`; never lifts an associate's GREEN-v-Test-nation. Settings → Form off.

- Head-to-head & friendlies: the pre-match "Two players" box sets `M.h2h {team: name}`, captains both sides and (optionally) real dice; prompts name the bowling side's player. `openFriendly` builds a one-off fixture (`fx.friendly`, key `F…`, any teams/format/date) started via `startFixture(key, fxObj)`; `onMatchDone`/`onMatchEvent` skip archive, stats, rankings, milestones and coach effects for `M.friendly`.

- Your own tournaments (`05c-cups.js`, `S.cups`, keys `C<n>.<k>`): `createCup` builds groups (snake seeding by rankings) and fixtures; knockout slots `S<n>`/`P<n>`/`A1`/`W:<key>` resolve in `resolveSlot` once the group stage or the feeding match is done (no result: the higher seed goes through); `cupFx` makes the fixture (unresolved = not playable; `fxDirty()` after every match so slots refresh); `tableFrom` is the shared points/NRR table; knockouts set `superOver`. Tests: league only. UI in `11-ui-tour.js` (`renderCup`, `openCupCreator`, `simCup` - one fixture at a time because slots resolve as you go).

- Stats lab (`07d-query.js`, UI `12e-ui-query.js`): `queryStats(q)` filters the archive by format, years, team, opponent, host (`VENUE_HOME`), batting position (index in `bt`), stage; `headToHead(batter, bowler, fmt)` reads per-innings matchups `inns[].mu` = `[batter, bowler, balls, runs, dismissals]` (added by `matchups(I)` in `archiveMatch`; older matches have none); `groundStats`. Player card: `nemesisHTML`.

## Layout
- `src/template.html` - page shell with `/*@@CSS@@*/`, `/*@@DATA@@*/`, `/*@@JS@@*/`
- `src/styles.css` - all CSS (Excel dark palette: navy `#0a0e1a`, panels `#0f172a`/`#1e293b`, cyan `#06b6d4`, gold `#ffd700`)
- `src/js/NN-*.js` - concatenated **in filename order into one global scope** (not modules): top-level names must be unique across files; `const`/`let` are subject to TDZ.
- `data/world.json` - generated, committed: teams, RR cards, fixtures, XIs, bowling plans, players (`[name, style P/S/M/N, rating 1-5 or 7, maxOvers, src h|d, batColour B/G/R/BG or '', batSrc h|d]`), optional `tests`/`txi`.
- `tools/extract.py` - workbook → `data/world.json` (needs `openpyxl`). Bowling ratings: the workbook's Players sheet (by name) wins, else derived from real BowlingXI spells (percentile of runs/over − 9×wkts/over vs full members; associates +1).
- `tools/cricsheet_tests.py` - adds real Tests from Cricsheet's `tests_json.zip` (stdlib only). Captain/keeper unknown in Cricsheet → -1, resolved in `testXI` from the nearest ODI XI.
- `tools/cricsheet_odis.py` - adds what really happened (`WORLD.real[cid]`: result, innings totals, top scorer, best bowling, player of the match) from Cricsheet's `odis_json.zip`. Game side `05b-history.js` (`realOf`, `sameAsHistory`, `timelineDrift`, `realChampion`, `realHTML`): shown only for matches you've played, never mid-match; Settings → Real results hides it.
- `tools/build.js [out] [world.json]`, `tools/check.js`, `tools/dev/` (eval helper, team-strength report)
- `tests/` - jsdom end-to-end tests `e2eN.js`, `engine-stats.js`, `runall.sh`, `seed.js`

## Commands
- `npm install`, `npm run build`, `npm test` (build + checks + all e2e + engine stats; ~30 s), `npm run check`
- `python3 tools/extract.py FirstXI.xlsm data/world.json` after changing the workbook data
- `python3 tools/cricsheet_tests.py tests_json.zip` to add the real Test calendar; `python3 tools/cricsheet_odis.py odis_json.zip` for real ODI results
- Reproducible: `FXI_SEED=42 node -r ./tests/seed.js tests/e2e1.js dist/first-xi.html`
- Engine tuning: `node tests/engine-stats.js 400 150`; balance: `node tools/dev/strength.js`

## Key architecture
- `01-util` helpers + save compression (`lzPack`/`lzUnpack`, `LZ1:` prefix, from Syndicate HQ). `02-world` indexes WORLD: `FIX` (ODIs, sorted), `TFIX` (real Tests), `SERIES` editions (same name, gaps < 60 days; Test editions prefixed `Test:`), `fixtureXI`, `testXI`, `nearestXI`, `squadAround`, `pAttr` (attribute edits in `S.attrEdits` win).
- `03-engine`: `newMatch` → `doToss` → `bowlBall(M, manualRoll)` (one delivery; returns `{need:'base'|'comp'}` when manual dice need a roll) → `closeInnings` → `nextPhase` → `finishMatch` → global hooks `onMatchEvent`/`onMatchDone`. `bowlOver`/`simInnings`/`simMatch` fast-forward. All match state is plain data in `M` (saved whole as `S.live`).
- `04-career`: `S` (see `newCareer`), `archiveMatch` (compact scorecard: `bt` rows `[pid,r,b,4s,6s,how,bowler,fielder,ballsAt50,ballsAt100]`, `bw` rows `[pid,balls,md,r,w,wd,nb]`, `parts`, `ov`), `applyStats`/`rebuildStats` (per format), milestones.
- `05-tournament`: `edition(sid)` (pools by pairing analysis - see rules below - stage per fixture), `pointsTable` (W4, T/NR 2, NRR with all-out = full overs), `groupTables`, `superTable` (carried points), timeline re-seeding `resolveFixture` (super stage: one-for-one swap inside each pool; knockouts: `resolveKO` SF 1v4/2v3 or A1vB2/B1vA2, QF A1vB4…), `seriesStatus`.
- `06-fixtures-save`: fixture keys `o<no>` (ODI), `r<no>` (real Test), `T<n>.<k>` (Test series you schedule, `S.tests`); `allFixtures` (cached), `buildMatch`, `onMatchDone` (archive → stats → milestones → save), `simFixture`; saves in localStorage `fxi_save_1..3`, slot in `fxi_slot`; ball logs kept for the last `LOG_KEEP` matches.
- `07-records`: `recordsData(fmt)` (cached by match count), qualification `REC_MIN`.
- UI (files before `13-…-init` so their consts exist when `init()` runs): `08-ui-shell` (nav/`go`/`RENDER`, modal, player card with rating edits), `09-ui-match` (pre-match XI swaps & toss, live view, dice panel, manual roll input, watch mode, scorecard, charts, RR card viewer, "what really happened"), `10-ui-home` (dashboard, bulk sim, Test series creator, fixtures), `11-ui-tour`, `12-ui-records` (records, players, results, milestones), `13-ui-settings-init` (settings, splash, live hooks, `init`).

## Rules learned the hard way (from the Excel version, still true)
- **Stage detection uses team-pairing analysis.** Never match counts (Cricsheet has gaps: 2003 World Cup is missing four games) and never fixture position.
- **Pool membership: both teams** in a fixture must be in the pool.
- **Pool size:** not raw Union-Find and not the opponent-count mode either (the mode breaks on gaps). `detectGroups` takes the largest cap whose capped Union-Find partition is a near-complete round robin (≥75% of in-pool pairs played).
- Labelled fixtures `(Match N)` are league/pool/super games; unlabelled ones are knockouts, except runs longer than QF+SF+F, whose early games are an unlabelled super stage (2003 Super Six).
- **Team IDs** are numbers everywhere (`"003"` → 3). Player ids are global (a player keeps his id across teams).
- **Milestones log live** during simulation, never approximated from post-match totals.

## Gotchas
- New persistent state goes in `newCareer()` and gets a default in `loadSlot` for old saves.
- Check for duplicate top-level names after adding code (`npm run check`).
- Adding a feature = a section in the most relevant `src/js` file (or a new `NN-name.js`), plus a test in `tests/e2eN.js`.
- Phones: `@media (max-width:600px)` at the end of `styles.css`; check new screens at 390px (no horizontal scroll).
- The workbook has no batting figures, so batting colours are proxies (position, caps, caps v full members). Change the thresholds only with `tools/dev/strength.js` (and check `tests/engine-stats.js` for Tests). Weak bowlers bowl the fast cards, which carry the most wickets (RR_7: 52 BLUE) - that's why bowling ratings alone couldn't separate the teams.
